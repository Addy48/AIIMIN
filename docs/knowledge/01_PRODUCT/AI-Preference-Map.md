# AI preference map — where users want AI vs where they refuse it

> Research pass 2026-08-20 (academic FinTech trust + GenAI privacy + CNBC/JFP finance advice findings). Combined with Genesis five AI roles (Offer / Analyzer / Coach — never silent writer).

## 1. Verdict

Users want AI as **bookkeeping + structure**, not as **authority over money, mood, or identity**.  
Trust rises with **privacy communication + provenance**; collapses when finance advice is opaque or journal text is “analyzed” without explicit Commit.

## 2. WANT AI (lean in — ship hard)

| Job | Why users like it | AIIMIN surface | Guardrail |
|-----|-------------------|----------------|-----------|
| **Structure messy Capture** | Typing “paid Zomato 240” → expense Offer | Capture / THE LINE | Offer → Adjust → Commit |
| **Categorize transactions** | Saves time | Money | Always editable; cite merchant rule |
| **Summarize week (with citations)** | Reports feel alive | Reports / Lab | Provenance drawer; no fake LIVE |
| **Habit / minimum reminders** | Knocks that earn attention | Knocks | Quiet hours; no coercion |
| **Parse receipts / UPI SMS drafts** | India-specific pain | Money review queue | Human Approve |
| **English practice scoring** | Objective feedback | Lab AEI | Deterministic metrics where possible |
| **OS-ID / onboarding help** | Less blank-page fear | Onboarding | No dark-pattern upsell |

## 3. TOLERATE AI (optional, default off or mid confidence)

| Job | Condition |
|-----|-----------|
| Mood phrasing suggestions | Only after explicit mood Settle |
| Goal wording / Arc language | User must Accept |
| Correlation “you sleep less → mood dips” | Server aggregate + cite; never from private journal body |
| Search / “find Mom loops” | Local-first graph before embeddings |

## 4. REJECT / HIGH-RESISTANCE (do not auto)

| Job | Evidence / why | AIIMIN rule |
|-----|----------------|-------------|
| **Autonomous investing / “buy X”** | Finance AI mistrusted; inconsistent advice across models (2026 studies) | No trade execution; no fiduciary theatre |
| **Silent journal reading for ads or scoring** | Privacy is fundamental resistance driver | Journal excluded from analytics (law) |
| **Diagnosing mental health** | Overclaim risk; US users misjudge HIPAA-like protections | Coach language only; no clinical labels |
| **Auto-writing Settles** | Breaks Capture grammar | Forbidden |
| **Predicting “you will relapse” without consent** | Shame + false precision | Discipline stays user-owned |
| **Sharing graph with friends by default** | Social forge = POST-V1 | Opt-in only |

## 5. Regional nuance

| Region | Lean |
|--------|------|
| **India** | Strong want: UPI parse, ₹ budgets, WhatsApp-share waitlist, family reminders. Suspicious of foreign LLM “knowing” private family money — keep on-device / confirm queues. |
| **US / Americas** | Strong want: Health Connect / Apple Health style passive BODY; calendar two-way. Strong refuse: AI money advice without disclosure; GenAI data reuse fears — need plain-language S&P transparency. |

## 6. Age nuance

| Age | AI posture |
|-----|------------|
| 18–24 | Higher tolerance for playful Coach; still hate silent money moves |
| 25–40 | Want time-savings AI; demand provenance |
| 40+ | Prefer fewer suggestions, larger controls, AI-off mode prominent |

## 7. Product checklist (anti-lie)

- [ ] Every insight has sources[]  
- [ ] AI-off mode usable for full Capture Life OS  
- [ ] Finance AI never invents balances  
- [ ] Waitlist / marketing copy never claims “AI therapist” or “AI CFO”
