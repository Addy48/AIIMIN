# Marketing & go-to-market — AIIMIN Life OS (production plan)

> Audience priority: **Indian subcontinent** then **US / Americas**. Brand: craft Life OS, not another purple AI dashboard.

## 1. Positioning (one sentence)

**AIIMIN is the personal Life OS that turns daily Capture into linked truth — habits, money, focus, mood, family — with AI that Offers structure and never silently rewrites your life.**

## 2. Category & enemies

| We are | We are not |
|--------|------------|
| Life OS / personal operating system | Habitica cosplay |
| Capture → Settle → Graph | Dashboard of widgets |
| Dual-region pricing (₹ + $) | One FX-converted price |
| Server Life Score (5D) | Fake client XP |

Competitive frame (education, not attack ads): Notion = docs · Finch = pet wellness · Habitica = RPG tasks · **AIIMIN = life graph + capture craft**.

## 3. Messaging pillars

1. **One Settle, many links** — expense tags a person, feeds MONEY, opens a loop.  
2. **Honest AI** — Offer / Adjust / Commit; provenance.  
3. **Built for India, ready for America** — UPI + IST defaults; USD catalog for US.  
4. **Founding member economics** — waitlist locks discounts (see [[Dual-Market-Pricing]]).  
5. **Native companion + web deep OS** — phone captures; desktop thinks.
