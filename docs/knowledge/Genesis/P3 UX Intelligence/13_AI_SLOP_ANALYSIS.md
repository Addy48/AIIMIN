## Slop inventory

### 1. Generic dark dashboard + orange accent
Locked palette is brand — good. Combined with card grids, rings, and LIVE badges it reads as **default AI startup dashboard 2024–26**. Accent inflation (orange on everything) kills accent meaning.

### 2. Glassmorphism clichés
Guest banner blur 24px; mobile bottom blur; brand sticky glass; Focus pills. Brand pillar says minimal decorative chrome — glass contradicts.

### 3. Hero metric / ring worship
Life Score ring is meaningful **if** honest. As layout, it matches every health/productivity app. Differentiation must be contributor narrative, not ring chrome.

### 4. Identical card grids
Family auto-fill cards; Overview week tiles; Settings mini stats; native More 2-col tiles — template repetition.

### 5. Emoji as personality substitute
Onboarding goals/habits; mood pickers; waitlist perks; demo wins; GuestTour wave. Emoji ≠ humanity. Conflicts with Absolute Precision pillar.

### 6. LIVE / pulse badges
Trajectory LIVE pulsing dot — dashboard theater.

### 7. Demo / placeholder as content
Recent Wins hardcoded samples — fake social proof of self.

### 8. Gradient marketing leftovers
GuestTour purple/green gradient cards — explicit anti-pattern vs locked brand (and user frontend-design rules against purple AI look).

### 9. Clone resonances
| Clone | Where felt |
|-------|------------|
| Notion | Notes/Lab modular everything; density |
| Linear | Issue-like Career pipeline; crisp ops tone |
| Apple Health | Rings / scores |
| Google Material | Native M3 nav/chips/FAB defaults |
| Generic AI wrapper | ⌘K “smart” log without visible craft |

### 10. Template interactions
Confirm dialogs ×18; tier modal ×8; multi-tab CRUD pages — necessary sometimes, identity-free often.

- Split brand lockup behavior
- ProductTour anti-cult phrasing

- OS-ID as identity ritual (when explained)
- Founder craft intentionality (Design Lab calling glass nav wrong)

## Why slop matters for five years

Users forgive unfinished features. They do not forgive **feeling replaceable**. Slop teaches “I could churn to any tracker.” Identity teaches “this is my OS.”

1. Remove/disable demo wins in prod paths
2. Kill GuestTour purple gradients / subscription lies
3. Demote LIVE badges; earn motion
4. Make Logger the sole primary magnet on Today
5. Replace emoji-grid onboarding with calmer choice patterns
6. Reduce glass to zero on product surfaces (keep brand page intentional if needed)
