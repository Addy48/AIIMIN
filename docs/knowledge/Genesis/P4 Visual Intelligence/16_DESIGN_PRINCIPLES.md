# 16 — Design Principles

## 1. Recognition without the logo

If the mark is cropped out, orange/green/ivory/charcoal + Arch geometry + type roles must still read AIIMIN.

## 2. Meaning before decoration

Color, motion, and elevation communicate state (act / done / danger / quiet). If a style has no meaning, remove it.

## 3. Orange acts; green completes

Never invert. Never replace with purple “AI.”

## 4. Warm paper, charcoal room

Light = human ivory. Dark = graphite control room. Not cool gray. Not void black as brand.

## 5. Capture cheap; command dense

Phone/native capture stays sparse. Desktop may be dense — but still one primary focus on Today.

## 6. Borders over carnival shadows

1px borders and tonal surfaces outrank stacked glass cards.

## 7. Type has jobs

Bodoni wordmark · Familjen ritual · Figtree product · JetBrains measure. No unemployed fonts.

## 8. Peak surfaces are rare

Focus cinematic, brand ember, celebration — scarce on purpose.

## 9. Structure creates personality

Page identity comes from layout rituals (studio, keep grid, score hero), not stickers.

## 10. Honesty is aesthetic

Life Score and streaks must look measured, not carnival. Sparring > sycophantic glow.

## 11. Anti-looks are principles too

Reject purple AI, cream-terracotta AI editorial, broadsheet density, GoodNotes PWA, therapist pastel.

## 12. Tokens are law; drift is debt

Multiple conflicting token sources are bugs, not “options.”

## 13. Motion earns its frames

Explain hierarchy or feedback. Otherwise stillness.

## 14. Accessibility is brand

Contrast locks, touch targets, reduced motion — not afterthoughts.

## 15. Timelessness > trend

Optimize for a company of designers in five years asking “what is AIIMIN?” — not for Dribbble week.
