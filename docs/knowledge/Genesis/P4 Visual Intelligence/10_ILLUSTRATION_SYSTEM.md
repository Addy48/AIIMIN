## Empty states

Tend toward Lucide + short copy. DESIGN.md wants empty states that **teach shortcuts** — content strategy more than illustration. Visual language still generic.

## AI graphics

No unique AI illustration system. Risk of Sparkles + purple gradients if Lab patterns leak.

## Decorative assets

Focus Room radial glows = emotional decoration with purpose (mode). Brand ember = brand. Glass mesh proto 12 = exploratory — do not promote to product DNA.

## Verdict

AIIMIN does **not** yet have a cohesive illustration system. Identity is carried by **mark + type + color + print report craft**, not by character illustrations.

That is acceptable if empty states stay typographic/editorial — **dangerous** if teams fill gaps with undraw/Storyset blobs or purple AI orbs.

## Board challenge

- **Illustration Director:** Prefer **diagrammatic / print / editorial** assets (report skins lineage) over cute SaaS mascots. Life OS ≠ fintech raccoon.
- **Creative Director:** Today fashion prototypes are inspiration for **composition**, not for shipping 12 visual themes.
