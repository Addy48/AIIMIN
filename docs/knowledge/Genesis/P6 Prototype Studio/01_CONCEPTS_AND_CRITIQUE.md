# 01 — Concepts and Critique

## Step 1 — Understanding (internal)

AIIMIN is not notes, not tasks, not a chat bot. It is a **Personal Life OS**: capture life as intent, structure into one graph, coach with honest signal, protect trust. Android is the first platform that must feel complete in the hand. Desktop inherits density later; phone web `/m` stays capture stopgap — native is not that ceiling.

Primary memory test: **Would someone remember this OS in a year?** Only if identity is unmistakable — Human Momentum, burnt orange on charcoal/ivory, capture-first calm, private trust — and IA does not collapse into a generic productivity suite.

---

## Step 2 — Twelve product concepts

### 1. Editorial Life Magazine
**Philosophy:** Life as curated magazine spreads.  
**Advantages:** Strong brand craft; memorable typography.  
**Disadvantages:** Slow capture; poor daily ops.  
**Risk:** Cream/terracotta AI-editorial monoculture (banned).  
**Uniqueness:** Medium.  
**Long-term:** Weak for OS; strong for marketing only.

### 2. Living Dashboard
**Philosophy:** Home = live widgets of every pillar.  
**Advantages:** Glanceable status.  
**Disadvantages:** Widget graveyard; cognitive tax.  
**Risk:** Becomes personal CRM; UX Context “pessimistic path.”  
**Uniqueness:** Low.  
**Long-term:** Dies under density.

### 3. AI-Native Copilot Home
**Philosophy:** Chat is the OS shell.  
**Advantages:** Future-facing; flexible.  
**Disadvantages:** Ambiguous state; clinical persona risk.  
**Risk:** Therapist trap; never-build.  
**Uniqueness:** Medium.  
**Long-term:** Feature inside OS, not the OS.

### 4. Minimal OS Shell
**Philosophy:** Near-empty canvas; power under gestures.  
**Advantages:** Calm; premium.  
**Disadvantages:** Discoverability; onboarding cliff.  
**Risk:** Feels unfinished to students under load.  
**Uniqueness:** High craft, low teachability.  
**Long-term:** Needs progressive disclosure.

### 5. Adaptive Context Surface
**Philosophy:** UI reshapes by time/location/energy.  
**Advantages:** Feels intelligent.  
**Disadvantages:** Unpredictable; hard to trust.  
**Risk:** Magic without exportable mental model.  
**Uniqueness:** High.  
**Long-term:** Layer later, not v1 spine.

### 6. Calm Computing
**Philosophy:** Quiet read surfaces; motion only for state; zero shame chrome.  
**Advantages:** Matches brand; anti-gamification anxiety.  
**Disadvantages:** Can feel “too soft” for builders who want command.  
**Risk:** Underpowered if capture is hidden.  
**Uniqueness:** Medium-high when paired with intent capture.  
**Long-term:** Excellent backbone.

### 7. Contextual Day (Ritual OS)
**Philosophy:** Morning / midday / night rituals drive structure.  
**Advantages:** Habit formation; one-screen day.  
**Disadvantages:** Rigid for chaotic schedules.  
**Risk:** Onboarding over-configuration.  
**Uniqueness:** Medium.  
**Long-term:** Strong as Today composition rule.

### 8. Relationship-First Family OS
**Philosophy:** Family vault and shared care as center.  
**Advantages:** Emotional differentiation; trust story.  
**Disadvantages:** Too narrow for solo student primary persona.  
**Risk:** Social-network gravity.  
**Uniqueness:** High in category.  
**Long-term:** Pillar, not whole product.

### 9. Memory-First Life Graph
**Philosophy:** Everything is memory; Timeline is truth; search is recall.  
**Advantages:** Compounding value; year-later memorability.  
**Disadvantages:** Weak without daily action surface.  
**Risk:** Archive app, not OS.  
**Uniqueness:** High.  
**Long-term:** Core differentiator when paired with Today.

### 10. Knowledge-First Second Brain
**Philosophy:** Notes/wiki as center.  
**Advantages:** Sticky for writers.  
**Disadvantages:** Notion clone trap.  
**Risk:** Identity collapse.  
**Uniqueness:** Low in 2026.  
**Long-term:** Subsystem only.

### 11. Energy-First Bio OS
**Philosophy:** Sleep/energy/sports gate the day.  
**Advantages:** Clear health narrative.  
**Disadvantages:** Fitness-app gravity; incomplete Life OS.  
**Risk:** Narrow graph.  
**Uniqueness:** Medium.  
**Long-term:** Signals inside Today/Timeline.

### 12. Intent Capture OS (Command Life)
**Philosophy:** Universal capture (type/voice) routes to entities; UI is consequence.  
**Advantages:** Matches doctrine; compression thesis.  
**Disadvantages:** Hard to visualize for new users.  
**Risk:** Invisible magic if feedback chips missing.  
**Uniqueness:** High when executed.  
**Long-term:** Essential interaction law.

## Step 3 — Critique (destroy weak)

### Kill immediately
| Concept | Kill reason |
|---------|-------------|
| Editorial Magazine | Banned aesthetic gravity; anti-capture |
| Living Dashboard | Explicit UX risk path |
| Knowledge-First | Notion clone |
| AI-Native Home | Clinical / chat-shell anti-pattern for v1 |
| Energy-First | Too narrow vs product graph |

### Keep for merge (top 3)
1. **Memory-First Life Graph** — year-later value
2. **Calm Computing + Contextual Day** — emotional/brand spine (merged as one for critique set)
3. **Intent Capture OS** — interaction law

*(Strict count of three survivors: Memory-First, Calm Day Ritual, Intent Capture.)*

### Role critiques (summary)
- **CPO:** Kill Tasks/Projects as primary IA — productivity category death.  
- **CDO / Brand:** Orange accent for action only; no purple AI glow.  
- **Principal UX:** Onboarding must be ≤3 beats to first capture.  
- **Behavioral psych:** No streak shame; celebrate action, not punishment.  
- **Accessibility:** 44px targets; AA contrast; reduced motion.  
- **Android UX:** Bottom nav ≤5; FAB = capture; sheets over modals.  
- **Design critic:** Prior opus prototype craft good; IA wrong (Tasks/Projects/Dashboard).

---

## Step 4 — Merge → AIIMIN v1: Living Momentum OS

### Definition
**Living Momentum OS** = Intent Capture + Calm Contextual Today + Memory/Timeline compound, with Family/Finance/Documents as trusted pillars and AI as mixed-initiative coach — never the home shell.

### Why
1. Passes the one-year memory test (identity + compounding memory).  
2. Honors Constitution / Product Bible / kill list.  
3. Scales: Android rituals → desktop command density without forking primitives.  
4. Differentiates from Notion/Linear/fitness/chat apps without novelty for novelty’s sake.

### Non-goals for v1 prototype
- Social feed, public leaderboards  
- Clinical mental health labels  
- Infinite customization  
- Widget marketplace  
- Separate Tasks/Projects worlds as primary nav
