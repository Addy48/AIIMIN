# 04 — Page Designs

Every required surface. Content is scenario-real (founder student builder), not lorem.

## Splash
Brand mark + AIIMIN wordmark + “Personal Life OS.” Auto-advances ~2.1s. Emotion: arrival, not loading spinner theater.

## Onboarding (3 beats)
1. **Capture once** — speak or type; system structures.  
2. **One graph** — habits, money, journal connect.  
3. **Honest mirror** — Life Score without shame.  
CTA: Continue / Begin. Skip available on last beat → Auth.

## Authentication
Google primary; email secondary. Trust microcopy: encrypted journal, export anytime. No purple OAuth chrome — branded buttons.

## Today
- Greeting + date  
- Life pulse ring (honest composite, not vanity XP)  
- Next focus block  
- Habit row (optimistic toggles)  
- Money pulse (today spend)  
- AI nudge card (coaching language, chip CTA)  
- No widget marketplace  

## Knowledge
Tabs: Journal | Notes.  
Journal: mood chip + prose capture.  
Notes: searchable cards; Enter to save.  
Structure after capture (tag chips appear post-save).

## Documents
File rows with type glyphs; upload FAB affordance in sheet; storage meta. Vault entry for sensitive IDs (PIN).

## Family
Member avatars; shared care items; “not a social feed” framing; vault lock entry.

## Finance
Today total; recent transactions; quick-add via capture (“spent 240 coffee” → chip confirm). Categories inferred.

## Timeline
Chronological day groups: captures, completions, spends, family events. Filter chips: All / Habits / Money / Memory.

## AI
Conversation + suggestion cards. Confidence chips. Explicit: coaching, not clinical. Shortcuts into Today / Finance / Knowledge.

## Search
Omnibox; results across notes, docs, events, projects-as-goals (labeled Goals, not Projects board). Recent queries.

## Settings
Account, appearance (theme), notifications, data sync/export, about, sign out.

## Profile
Identity, OS-ID, month consistency (honest), membership tier. Achievements restrained — action-based, not shame streaks carnival.

## Notifications (supporting)
Grouped Today / Earlier; mark all read.
