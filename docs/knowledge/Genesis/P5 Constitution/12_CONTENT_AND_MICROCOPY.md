## Purpose

Define how AIIMIN speaks — tone, verbs, coaching language, error voice, and empty-state teaching — as a permanent editorial system.

## Reasoning

Sparring over sycophancy, non-clinical coaching, and pattern language are product locks that live in words as much as widgets. Microcopy is behavior design.

## Evidence

Product Principles (no clinical claims; empty states teach); Discipline pattern language; Brand Human Momentum; Never-to-Build therapist persona; Confirm patterns.

---

## Voice

| Trait | Means | Not |
|-------|-------|-----|
| Calm | Steady, short, clear | Hype, exclamation addiction |
| Precise | Exact verbs and outcomes | Vague “magic happened” |
| Honest | Admits uncertainty; shows data | Flattery; fake certainty |
| Human | Respects stress and time | Corporate wallpaper |
| Sparring | Challenges weak habits with evidence | Insults; moralizing |

## Editorial laws

1. **Coach, don’t diagnose.** No disorder labels, no therapist persona.
2. **Pattern over shame.** Prefer “Tuesday evenings correlate with urges” over “you failed your streak.”
3. **Orange is action, not alarm.** Error/destructive copy uses clarity, not accent-as-panic.
4. **Short by default.** One sentence when one sentence works.
5. **Name the next action.** Buttons and empty states say what happens.
6. **Admit inference.** “Categorized as Food — change” beats silent certainty.
7. **Never hostage.** Upgrade copy informs; it does not trap.
8. **Privacy in words.** Do not preview journal body in push notifications.
9. **Consistent verbs.** See Naming Language.
10. **Teach in emptiness.** Empty states include how (shortcut/path), not only what is missing.

## Microcopy patterns

| Situation | Pattern |
|-----------|---------|
| Save success | Quiet confirmation; no parade |
| Inference chip | Value + affordance to edit |
| Destructive | Consequence + confirm verb |
| Permission | Why + what we won’t do |
| Coaching | Observation → optional action link |
| Error | What failed + how to retry |
| Loading | Honest wait; sync pending named |

## Words we avoid

Therapist, diagnose, addict (as identity), shame-based streak threats, “just,” engagement spam (“Don’t miss out!”), fake urgency, clinical DSM language, “AI magic.”

## Words we prefer

Log, capture, review, connect, continue, edit, confirm, export, delete, pattern, momentum, Life Score, replace (habit), pause (Focus).

## Localization posture (future)

Principles travel; idioms get rewritten. Tone traits stay even when language changes.

## Future impact

Voice/AR prompts inherit the same voice. New channels do not get a louder carnival copy deck.

## Tradeoffs

Sparring can feel less “nice” than sycophantic apps. Honesty is the brand.

## Known risks

- Insights LLM drift into clinical tone — needs output filters and examples.
- Marketing pages using banned hype while product stays calm.
- Inconsistent button verbs across legacy screens.
