## Purpose

State, in one place, what AIIMIN *is* as a designed product — not as a codebase, not as a roadmap, and not as a set of screens. This summary is the shortest path into the Constitution and Design Bible.

## Reasoning

Phases before this produced intelligence: repository map, knowledge doctrine, product bible, interaction audit, kill list, research foundations. Intelligence without synthesis becomes archive. This package converts that archive into **permanent design law** so future designers can build without reopening foundational arguments.

## The twenty-year answer

**If AIIMIN exists in twenty years, these must still be true:**

1. AIIMIN is a **Personal Life OS** — one graph of a person's life, not twelve apps glued together.
2. The human **expresses intent**; the system **structures** — capture first, structure later.
3. **One primitive, many surfaces** — mood, money, journal, goals never fork into duplicate UIs.
4. **AI is mixed-initiative** — acts when confident, suggests when uncertain, asks when necessary; never clinical, never silent-wrong.
5. **Trust is the product** — export, delete, encryption, no dark patterns, no social feed.
6. **Identity is locked** — Human Momentum; burnt orange on charcoal/ivory; mark → brand, wordmark → Today.
7. **Calm command surfaces** — read surfaces stay quiet; capture surfaces stay fast; motion only communicates.
8. **Device roles stay honest** — phone web captures; desktop commands; native is a rich companion, not a crippled `/m`.
9. **Honesty over vanity** — Life Score mirrors reality; XP celebrates action; shame is never a retention strategy.
10. **Every feature must justify itself** against a human problem, a compression thesis, and the kill list.

## Dependencies

- Product Bible remains product doctrine; this Bible is **design constitution**. Where they overlap, they must agree. Conflicts resolve toward Constitution + locked product decisions.
- Palette.md remains the color authority.
- Vault Brain OS remains operational memory for agents; this Bible is foundational law.

## Future impact

Any redesign, new surface, new client, or new AI capability must pass through this Bible before implementation debate begins. Redesigns may change chrome; they may not change constitution without explicit founder override.

## Tradeoffs

| Choice | Cost |
|--------|------|
| Timeless principles over UI recipes | Less immediate “how to draw this screen” |
| Behavior blueprints without styling | Designers still need craft judgment within locks |
| Synthesis of imperfect prior packages | Some UX/Visual gaps inferred, not measured |

## Known risks

- Treating this as a style guide instead of a constitution.
- Re-litigating locks (palette, nav, `/m`, clinical boundary) under trend pressure.
- Expanding “Knowledge / Documents / Timeline” page names without mapping to shipped routes — blueprints use canonical product surfaces and aliases.
