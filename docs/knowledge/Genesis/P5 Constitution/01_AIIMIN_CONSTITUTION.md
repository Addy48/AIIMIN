## Purpose

Define the immutable principles of AIIMIN — what it is, what it refuses to become, what success and failure mean — so that decades of redesigns cannot erase identity.

## Reasoning

Products die when redesigns rewrite purpose. AIIMIN already survived blue→orange, Clerk→Better Auth, leaf→Arch Bracket, WaitlistBrand→Human Momentum, and GoodNotes rejection. Survivors share one trait: they protected a coherent idea of the product. This Constitution encodes that idea as law.

## Evidence

Product Bible Vision & Philosophy; Knowledge Context Product Philosophy; Decisions D-PAL, D-NAV, D-MWEB, D-CLIN, D-JENC; Never-to-Build list; Design History survivors/deaths.

## Article I — Why AIIMIN exists

AIIMIN exists so a person under cognitive load can:

1. **Capture life as it happens** without becoming a data-entry clerk.
2. **See one connected graph** of goals, habits, money, calendar, body, mind, family, and work.
3. **Act tomorrow with less friction** than today — through memory, connection, and honest coaching.

Vision lock: *Capture once. AIIMIN remembers, connects, and coaches — without turning life into data entry.*

Brand frame: **Human Momentum** — precision, feedback loops, behavioral intelligence, data sovereignty, momentum engineering — never shame theater.

## Article II — What AIIMIN is

AIIMIN is a **Personal Life OS**.

| Is | Means |
|----|--------|
| Personal | One human’s life graph; not a network |
| Life | Whole person: execution, money, health, reflection, family, career |
| OS | Shared primitives, shared memory, shared intelligence across surfaces |

AIIMIN is **AI-first** in the sense that intent is expressed in natural language and structure is derived — not in the sense that a chatbot is the product.

## Article III — What AIIMIN will never become

1. A social network, public feed, or public leaderboard.
2. A clinical mental health device, diagnostic tool, or AI therapist.
3. A single-domain app (finance-only, fitness-only, notes-only) wearing Life OS clothing.
4. A form builder disguised as productivity.
5. A gamification casino that optimizes for streaks and shame.
6. A purple SaaS clone, cream-terracotta editorial AI look, or GoodNotes handwriting PWA.
7. A product that sells or shares lifelog data.
8. A product that blocks capture behind mode pickers, category interrogations, or vanity configuration.

## Article IV — What AIIMIN refuses to compromise

| Refusal | Rationale |
|---------|-----------|
| Palette identity | Recognition requires continuity |
| Split brand lockup (mark→`/brand`, wordmark→Today) | Brand book ≠ daily OS |
| Capture-first on phone web `/m` | Device honesty |
| No clinical claims | Ethics and liability |
| Journal body out of analytics | Privacy |
| Export and delete always available | Trust |
| Destructive actions confirm | Safety |
| Vault ships with behavior change | Institutional memory |
| Auth/schema only with explicit ask | Safety for agents and humans |
| One linking system for life entities | Graph integrity |

## Article V — What defines AIIMIN

1. **Intent over interface** — users arrive with needs, not taxonomies.
2. **Capture first, structure later** — raw expression is highest-fidelity signal.
3. **One primitive, many surfaces** — no duplicate mood/theme/arc/resume UIs.
4. **Mixed-initiative partnership** — AI confidence bands gate action.
5. **Read surfaces stay calm** — intelligence without interrogation.
6. **Sparring over sycophancy** — challenge weak habits with data.
7. **Honesty of Life Score** — composite truth, not vanity XP.
8. **Compression as craft** — median daily interactions trend down, not up.

## Article VI — What success means

Success is not downloads, DAU vanity, or feature count.

Success is:

- Daily capture possible in under ~60 seconds when the user is ready.
- Median meaningful interactions per active day trending toward ~5, not ~15+.
- User can answer “how am I doing?” without visiting four apps.
- User trusts the system enough to write privately and still export everything.
- Tomorrow’s plan is lighter because yesterday was remembered.
- Designers and agents can ship without asking “who are we?”

## Article VII — What failure means

Failure is:

- Rising daily interaction count for the same human outcomes.
- Duplicate primitives proliferating.
- Clinical or therapist framing.
- Analytics tools on phone web `/m`.
- Social comparison as growth strategy.
- Silent wrong automation without correction path.
- Feature shipped without a human problem.
- Redesign that could belong to another brand after removing the logo.

## Article VIII — What users should feel

Calm. Capable. Honest. Respected. In momentum — not in debt to the product.

Primary emotional contract: **relief after capture**, **clarity after review**, **agency after coaching**.

## Article IX — What users should never feel

Interrogated. Shamed. Trapped. Surveilled. Confused by decoration. Patronized by “AI magic.” Forced to choose among five moods or fourteen lab modules to do one simple thing.

## Article X — What the product optimizes for

| Optimize | Deliberately avoid |
|----------|--------------------|
| Capture speed | Configuration theater |
| Connected memory | Siloed widgets |
| Correctable inference | Silent wrongness |
| Interruptibility respect | JITAI nag loops |
| Pattern language | Punitive streaks |
| Progressive disclosure by stakes | Same friction everywhere |
| Long-term trust | Short-term engagement hacks |
| Timeless identity | Trend following |

## Article XI — Amendment

Constitutional change requires **explicit founder approval**, written ADR or decision log entry, vault update, and Design Bible revision with changelog. Agents and redesign sprints may not silently amend Articles I–X.

## Dependencies

Product Bible; Palette.md; Product Decisions locks; Never-to-Build.

## Future impact

All principles, blueprints, and growth rules in this Bible derive from these Articles. If a later principle contradicts an Article, the Article wins until amended.

## Tradeoffs

Immutability slows opportunistic pivots. That is intentional. AIIMIN’s threat is identity drift, not insufficient novelty.

## Known risks

- Over-interpreting “AI-first” as chatbot-first.
- Treating native capture-richness as permission to break phone-web `/m` lock.
- Using “Human Momentum” as slogan without behavioral honesty (Life Score, sparring).
