## Purpose

Explain how AIIMIN thinks — the worldview behind Life, Knowledge, Memory, Goals, Relationships, Planning, Reflection, and AI — without describing implementation.

## Reasoning

Features without philosophy become inventory. AIIMIN’s differentiation is not “another dashboard”; it is a stance about how a human and a system share cognitive labor.

## Evidence

Product Bible Vision/Philosophy/Human Problems/Information Model/AI Model; Knowledge Context Product Philosophy; Future AIIMIN Framework; kill list compression thesis.

---

## How AIIMIN thinks

AIIMIN assumes:

1. Humans arrive with **intents**, not taxonomies.
2. Structure is a **service**, not a ritual the user must perform before being heard.
3. Life is a **graph**, not a folder tree.
4. Intelligence earns trust by being **correctable**, interruptible, and non-clinical.
5. The product’s job is **momentum**, not completeness theater.

## What makes AIIMIN different

| Axis | Typical apps | AIIMIN |
|------|--------------|--------|
| Input | Form per domain | Utterance → router → entities |
| Memory | Per-app silos | One life graph |
| Coaching | Generic tips / clinical tone | Pattern language + honest score |
| Mobile | Shrink the desktop | Capture-first web; rich native companion |
| AI | Chat overlay | Mixed-initiative layer with confidence bands |
| Growth | Social / streaks | Trust + compression |

## Philosophy of Life

Life is continuous. The OS should not force the user to pretend life is a project management board. Capture nodes feed execution nodes; execution feeds reflection; reflection feeds planning. The loop is the product.

## Philosophy of Knowledge

Knowledge in AIIMIN is **source-grounded reference**, not a second journal and not a handwriting canvas. Notes exist to hold durable references and links into the life graph. Knowledge surfaces must answer “what do I know / what have I saved?” without competing with “how do I feel?”

## Philosophy of Memory

Memory is the system’s obligation. The user should not re-enter the same facts across finance, journal, goals, and family. Memory includes:

- Entity memory (who, what, where)
- Temporal memory (when, sequences, streaks as facts not shame)
- Relational memory (edges between entities)

Forgetting is a product bug when the data existed. Inventing memory is a trust crime.

## Philosophy of Goals

Goals are **direction**, not forms. Priority is better inferred from behavior than demanded as a dropdown. Milestones are proposals the user owns. Goals must connect to habits, calendar, and focus — or they are decorations.

## Philosophy of Relationships (Family)

Family in AIIMIN is **care infrastructure**, not social networking. Emergency readiness, documents, and member context justify high stakes and progressive wizards. Daily flows stay light; crisis setup earns more fields. Never infer medications or allergies.

## Philosophy of Planning

Planning is the translation of direction into time. Calendar and focus are execution surfaces. Morning briefing should merge plan — not invent a second planning religion. Planning UI that increases daily interactions without increasing follow-through is failure.

## Philosophy of Reflection

Reflection must start empty of gates. Mode pickers before writing kill vent intent. Structure (CBT tags, themes, mood) arrives after capture or as optional enrichment. Reflection surfaces stay calm; they do not compete with capture urgency.

## Philosophy of Money

Money logging must respect the moment of spend: utterance first, fields later. Categories are inferred; chips correct. Budgets and reports are read intelligence, not daily interrogation.

## Philosophy of Habits & Discipline

Habits are toggles of commitment — protect low friction. Discipline uses **pattern language**, not addiction scores or therapist personas. Urge notes stay private without consent. Interruptibility is sacred during Focus.

## Philosophy of AI

AI is five roles: Router, Inferencer, Analyzer, Coach, Composer.

| Confidence | Behavior |
|------------|----------|
| High (≥70%) | Act; leave edit chip |
| Medium (40–70%) | Pre-fill; require confirm |
| Low (<40%) | Ask the minimum |
| Safety / legal | Never infer — always ask |

AI must not diagnose, auto-share journal, invent finance transactions, change auth/billing, or block capture.

## Philosophy of Information organization

Organize by **human intent and life graph**, not by engineering tables or vendor modules. Navigation is user-owned (free-pin), not a forced taxonomy sermon. Read surfaces consolidate (insights into reports) rather than multiply dashboards.

## Future impact

Every feature proposal must map to a philosophy domain above and state which belief it advances.

## Tradeoffs

Graph thinking costs more engineering than siloed apps. Philosophy forbids “just add another page” as the default solution.

## Known risks

- “Knowledge” and “Documents” product language drifting into vague marketing without graph anchors.
- AI Coach drifting into therapy voice under retention pressure.
- Planning surfaces reintroducing configuration before capture.
