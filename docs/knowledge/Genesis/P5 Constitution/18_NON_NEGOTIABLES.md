## Purpose

Enumerate permanent rules — required behaviors and forbidden moves — that do not depend on sprint taste.

## Reasoning

Product Bible Never-to-Build and Decision locks already proved their value (blue death, therapist ban, `/m` ceiling). Non-negotiables expand those into a design-operating checklist.

## Evidence

Constitution; Principles 1–20; Kill List; Decisions D-*; Interaction audit duplicates; Design History deaths.

---

## Required (must)

1. Every feature justifies a human problem.
2. Every interaction justifies fidelity, speed, trust, or clarity.
3. Every animation communicates meaning.
4. Every notification deserves attention.
5. Every destructive action confirms (branded).
6. Every inference offers correction (chip or edit).
7. Every capture path allows Enter/primary save without ceremony.
8. Every empty state teaches.
9. Every behavior change updates vault (and Bible if contractual).
10. Export and delete remain available.
11. Journal body stays out of analytics.
12. Palette identity remains locked until founder override.
13. Brand lockup stays split.
14. Phone web `/m` stays capture-only.
15. Life Score stays honest; XP stays celebratory — roles unmixed.
16. Clinical claims stay absent.
17. Auth/schema changes require explicit human ask.
18. Secrets never enter vault/docs/chat dumps for commit.
19. One write primitive per concept.
20. Focus remains interruptibility-protected.
21. Meds/allergies/PIN never inferred.
22. Accessibility contrasts and operable capture on critical paths.
23. Reduced motion honored.
24. Cross-client token meaning stays aligned.
25. Kill List consulted before new fields.

## Gate questions (print beside every proposal)

1. Kill List conflict?
2. Duplicate primitive?
3. Increases daily interactions for same outcome?
4. Device ceiling respected?
5. Can AI infer with chip instead of ask?
6. Still true in ten years?
7. Trust pillars intact?
8. Constitution article conflict?

Any fail → redesign or reject.

## Future impact

This is the fastest enforcement tool for agents and humans. When uncertain, Non-Negotiables beat taste.

## Tradeoffs

Long refuse lists feel constraining. Constraint is how AIIMIN remains itself.

## Known risks

- Treating list as complete — amend when new failure modes appear, via founder process.
- Weaponizing list to block all experimentation — Design Lab remains valid sandboxed exploration.
