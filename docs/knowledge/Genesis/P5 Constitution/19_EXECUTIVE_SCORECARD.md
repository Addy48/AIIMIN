## Purpose

Provide a grading instrument for design fidelity — so leaders and agents can score whether a proposal, sprint, or shipped surface still is AIIMIN.

## Reasoning

Without a scorecard, Constitution becomes wallpaper. Scores make philosophy operational. This is evaluation, not vanity metrics of growth hacking.

## Evidence

Constitution success/failure articles; compression north star; trust pillars; brand test; Non-Negotiables.

---

## How to use

Score each dimension **0–5**:

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 5 | Exemplary; teaches others |
| 4 | Solid fidelity |
| 3 | Acceptable with notes |
| 2 | Drift; needs correction |
| 1 | Violates spirit |
| 0 | Violates Non-Negotiable / Constitution |

**Ship bar:** no dimension at 0–1; average ≥3.5; brand test must pass (≥4).

**Founder override** required to ship with any 0–1.

---

## Dimensions

### D1 — Constitutional fidelity

Does this uphold why AIIMIN exists and what it refuses to become?

### D2 — Capture-first

Does the primary path express intent before structure?

### D3 — Primitive integrity

One write primitive per concept? No duplicate pickers/editors?

### D4 — Compression

Net daily interactions for the outcome ↓ or justified by stakes?

### D5 — Mixed-initiative quality

Confidence bands, chips, no silent wrongness, no Focus ambush?

### D6 — Trust & privacy

Export/delete, journal safety, confirms, no dark patterns?

### D7 — Device honesty

Ceilings respected (`/m`, native, desktop)?

### D8 — Visual identity

Palette, lockup, anti-looks, brand test?

### D9 — Calm premium craft

Motion purposeful; clutter absent; cards earned?

### D10 — Accessibility

Contrast, keyboard/touch, reduced motion, labelled controls?

### D11 — Content voice

Calm, precise, honest, non-clinical, sparring-not-shame?

### D12 — Blueprint alignment

Matches page/component blueprint purpose and hierarchy?

---

## Scenario scorecards (templates)

### A — New feature proposal

Score D1–D6, D12 before design starts. Gate on Non-Negotiables checklist.

### B — Visual redesign

Score D8–D11 heavily; D1–D3 must not regress.

### C — AI capability

Score D5, D6, D11 mandatory; clinical ban auto-fail.

### D — New platform surface

Score D7 + Growth Rules gate; then D1–D6.

## North-star product signals (complementary)

Not design scores alone — track alongside:

| Signal | Healthy direction |
|--------|-------------------|
| Median daily capture interactions | ↓ toward ~5 |
| Time to first capture (new user) | ↓ |
| Journal funnel completion | ↑ without mode gates |
| Duplicate primitive count | ↓ |
| ConfirmDialog coverage vs window.confirm | ↑ |
| Brand test blind review | pass |
