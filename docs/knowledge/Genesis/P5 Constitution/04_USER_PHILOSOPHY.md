## Purpose

Define who AIIMIN serves, the trust model, mental models, identity relationship, habits, and motivation — so design choices serve a real human, not an abstract “user.”

## Reasoning

Without a user philosophy, products optimize for personas that flatter the builder. AIIMIN’s documented primary audience is high-cognitive-load builders (students / early-career) who want measurable routines and recovery loops — practical coaching over vanity analytics.

## Evidence

Product Guide / Product.md audience; Human Problems; User Journey; privacy principles (journal encryption, export/delete); Device tiers; Native vs `/m` decisions.

---

## Who AIIMIN serves

### Primary user

A person under **high cognitive load** who:

- Juggles goals, money, focus, health signals, and reflection.
- Will abandon tools that interrogate before listening.
- Wants a mirror that is honest, not a coach that flatters.
- Needs mobile capture in the wild and deeper command on larger screens.
- Values privacy of journal and discipline notes.

Archetype shorthand: **builder under load** — not “productivity influencer,” not “clinical patient,” not “social poster.”

### Secondary users

| Secondary | Relationship |
|-----------|--------------|
| Founder/operators configuring family vault | High-stakes rare flows |
| Career-active users (placements) | Domain depth without becoming LinkedIn |
| Future household collaborators (if ever) | Must not become a social network; collaboration ≠ feed |
| Agents / designers / developers | Consumers of this Bible and vault |

Secondary needs never rewrite primary: capture speed and trust remain supreme.

## Long-term relationship

AIIMIN aims for a **years-long operating system relationship**, not a 30-day habit challenge.

Phases of relationship:

1. **Activation** — first capture fast; onboarding compressed.
2. **Stabilization** — daily loop becomes muscle memory.
3. **Deepening** — graph connections and Life Score become meaningful.
4. **Sovereignty** — user can export, delete, and leave without hostage data.

The product earns the right to coach only after it earns the right to remember.

### Five-year path (UX Intelligence)

| Path | Experience |
|------|------------|
| Optimistic (doctrine wins) | One morning hub; capture &lt;60s; honest Life Score; private archive compounds; devices share one story with honest ceilings |
| Pessimistic (density wins) | Activation gauntlet; personal CRM of widgets; five mood UIs; phone/desktop/native teach three IAs; product becomes a second job |
| Current trajectory | Hybrid — power users who find Logger/⌘K/toggles stay; casual users bounce on waitlist→PIN→long onboarding or device story mismatch |

Design must actively choose the optimistic path.

## Trust model

Trust compounds from:

| Pillar | User expectation |
|--------|------------------|
| Confidentiality | Journal body never in analytics; urge notes consent-gated |
| Controllability | Correction chips; confirm destructive; no silent money invent |
| Portability | Export and delete always |
| Honesty | Life Score truth; no fake clinical certainty |
| Predictability | Automation respects confidence bands and Focus |
| Non-manipulation | No dark upgrade loops; no shame streaks as growth |

Break any pillar and retention tactics become irrelevant.

## Privacy expectations

- Personal lifelog is not advertising fuel.
- Inference is a convenience with undo, not surveillance cosplay.
- Safety fields (meds, allergies, PIN) are never inferred.
- Telemetry hashes PII; never logs PIN.
- On-device preference where passive sensing exists; HealthKit-class inputs are opt-in.

## Mental models

Users should be able to hold these models without training manuals:

1. **Today** = what matters now + capture.
2. **Say it once** = Command Palette / Universal Logger routes it.
3. **Graph** = things connect; I do not retype.
4. **Review** = calm read surfaces tell me patterns.
5. **Settings** = rare; defaults are sane.
6. **Brand** = philosophy page, not the daily OS.

If a feature requires a new mental model, it must replace an old one — not stack a twelfth.

## Identity

AIIMIN supports **self-authorship** (Life Arc, profile, pillars) without identity performance for an audience. Identity tools are private instruments. Public brand presence (`/brand`) is manifesto, not profile page.

## Habits

Habit philosophy for the user:

- Small truthful actions beat elaborate rituals.
- Toggles should be the easiest honest act in the product.
- Streaks are facts available for reflection — not weapons.
- Replacement behaviors beat prohibition language.

## Motivation

AIIMIN motivates through:

| Mechanism | Use |
|-----------|-----|
| Momentum / completion feedback | Immediate, local, honest |
| XP | Celebrate action |
| Life Score | Inform truth across pillars |
| Pattern insights | Invite change without shame |
| Sparring copy | Challenge weak assumptions with data |

AIIMIN does **not** motivate through public ranking, fear of breaking streaks, or therapist authority.

## Future impact

Personas for research must map to this philosophy. New segments (parents, teams) require Constitutional review before social or clinical features sneak in.

## Tradeoffs

Narrow primary user clarity can feel exclusive. Breadth without philosophy produces Notion-for-everything mush. AIIMIN chooses clarity.

## Known risks

- Optimizing for power users until beginners cannot activate.
- Optimizing for activation until power users lose command density.
- Confusing “builder under load” with “only STEM students forever.”
