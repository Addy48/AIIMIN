## Purpose

Define how AIIMIN expands across Desktop, Tablet, Watch, Car, Voice, Wearables, AR, and future AI — without losing identity.

## Reasoning

Device tiers already encode a truth: not every surface deserves every feature. Growth without rules recreates app fragmentation inside one brand. Constitution must travel; pixels may adapt.

## Evidence

Device-Tiers; Native ≠ `/m`; Future AIIMIN Framework passive sensing; Motion/Visual principles; Product Bible future ideas.

---

## Growth axiom

**Identity is invariant. Modality is variable. Ceiling is intentional.**

Every new surface must declare:

1. Which Constitutional articles it upholds.
2. Its capture vs command vs review ceiling.
3. Which component contracts it reuses.
4. What it will **never** do (surface-specific non-negotiables).

## Platform rules

### Desktop (web ≥1100 / large windows)

- Full Life OS command surface.
- Palette and keyboard mastery thrive.
- Density may rise; calm review must remain available.
- Do not assume hover-only essential actions.

### Tablet (768–1099 class)

- Full OS with TabRail-class navigation.
- Touch targets and density balance.
- Not a blown-up phone capture shell; not a cramped desktop.

### Phone web (`/m`)

- Capture-only remains law.
- Growth here means better capture, better offline honesty — not analytics smuggling.

### Native mobile

- Rich companion allowed; must still feel like same Life OS (tokens, voice, primitives).
- Outbox/sync honesty mandatory.
- Biometrics gate is security UX, not engagement gimmick.

### Watch

- Capture pulses and glances only: toggle, quick log, timer status, score glance.
- No multi-step finance forms.
- Complications show truth, not vanity spam.
- Confirm destructive only if truly necessary; prefer phone handoff for high stakes.

### Car

- Eyes-free / glance-safe: voice capture, read-backs, next calendar block.
- No dense dashboards while driving.
- Safety > completeness; delay complex review until parked (system/OS rules first).

### Voice

- Same intent router philosophy as Palette.
- Always provide typed/visual correction path when screen exists.
- Speak coaching sparingly; never clinical.
- Notifications-as-speech must still “deserve attention.”

### Wearables / passive sensing

- Opt-in only.
- On-device preference where possible.
- Infer-then-chip remains law.
- Never silent clinical labeling from sensors.
- Health data is context for Life OS, not a fitness-app identity takeover.

### AR

- Context overlays for capture and memory retrieval — not social AR network.
- Minimal chrome; real-world attention wins.
- Identity tokens as subtle recognition, not neon mesh cosplay.
- High-stakes family/medical never AR-inferred.

### Future AI

- Roles remain Router/Inferencer/Analyzer/Coach/Composer.
- Confidence bands remain.
- New model power does not unlock therapist persona, auto-posting, or invented money.
- Agents inside AIIMIN obey vault + Constitution; they do not invent product law.

## Design system evolution rules

1. **Extend tokens by role, not by trend palette.**
2. **Add modality blueprints; do not fork Constitutions per device.**
3. **Prefer responsive contracts over separate product lines** unless founder creates a new product.
4. **Kill list applies to new modalities** — do not reintroduce dead fields on watch because space is tiny (tiny space means fewer fields, not worse fields).
5. **Marketing surfaces may innovate within anti-look bans; OS surfaces stay restrained.**
6. **When two clients disagree, tokens + blueprints + Constitution arbitrate — not whichever shipped last.**

## Expansion decision gate

Before building a new platform surface, answer:

1. What human problem is modality-unique here?
2. Which page blueprint roles transfer?
3. What is explicitly out of ceiling?
4. How is trust preserved (privacy, confirm, export)?
5. Will this still be true in ten years, or is it a gadget demo?

If gate fails → prototype in Design Lab only; do not ship identity risk.

## Future impact

This document is the passport for multi-device AIIMIN. Without it, success on one device becomes fragmentation on five.

## Tradeoffs

Saying no to “full app on watch” forgoes feature parity marketing. Parity without ceilings destroys calm.

## Known risks

- Native feature envy breaking `/m` law via web ports.
- Voice coach becoming therapist.
- Wearable vendors pushing clinical claims.
- AR novelty violating anti-look and attention ethics.
