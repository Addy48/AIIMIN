## Purpose

Define how AIIMIN shapes behavior — ethically and effectively — using cognitive and behavioral science without becoming a clinical product or engagement dark-pattern machine.

## Reasoning

AIIMIN explicitly rejected shame-streak products, AI therapist personas, and proactive JITAI discipline nudges that violate interruptibility. Behavioral philosophy must keep the useful science (urge surfing, habit loops, feedback, progressive disclosure) while discarding coercive applications.

## Evidence

Discipline ADR (UrgeEvent + pattern language); Research Foundations; Kill List; Automation Rules; Interaction compression targets; Product Principles (sparring, interruptibility).

---

## Intended life loop (UX Intelligence)

```text
Capture → Score → Insight → Action → Capture
```

Each arrow must stay low-friction and non-shaming. North-star retention signal: **Weekly Active Capture (WAC)** among activated users — not raw DAU theater.

## Core behavioral stance

AIIMIN is a **behavioral partner**, not a behavioral overlord.

| Partner does | Overlord does |
|--------------|---------------|
| Makes the next honest action easier | Forces rituals |
| Reflects patterns | Diagnoses disorders |
| Suggests replacements | Punishes failure |
| Respects attention windows | Interrupts for “engagement” |
| Uses data to spar | Uses guilt to retain |

## Cognitive load doctrine

The primary user already carries high load. Therefore:

1. **Reduce decisions** at capture time.
2. **Defer structure** until after expression.
3. **Prefer recognition over recall** (chips, defaults, history).
4. **One primary action** per moment.
5. **Batch rare complexity** into wizards (family emergency), never into daily loops.

Compression target (north star): median daily capture interactions ~15 → ~5 without losing fidelity.

## Motivation architecture

### Dual score honesty

- **XP** = celebration of actions taken.
- **Life Score** = cross-pillar truth.

Never let XP cosmetics rewrite Life Score meaning. Never let Life Score become a vanity badge.

### Feedback timing

| Timing | Use |
|--------|-----|
| Immediate | Toggle, save, chip confirm |
| Session | Focus completion, capture relief |
| Periodic | Weekly digest, reports |
| Never | Mid-Focus modal coaching; surprise clinical labels |

## Habit & discipline ethics

1. Habit completion remains a **low-friction truthful act**.
2. Discipline uses **pattern language** (triggers, replacements, contexts) — not addiction scoring.
3. Urge notes are **consent-gated** for AI.
4. Proactive JITAI-style nudges that chase the user across contexts are rejected or deferred until interruptibility is solved.
5. Streaks may be shown as information; they must not be weaponized.

## Persuasion boundaries

Allowed:

- Defaults that favor capture.
- Pre-fills with correction.
- Sparring copy grounded in the user’s own data.
- Progressive commitment (start small, deepen later).

Forbidden:

- Dark patterns on upgrade.
- Fake urgency.
- Social proof humiliation.
- Infinite customization disguised as empowerment.
- “Complete your profile” walls that block capture.

## Attention & interruptibility

Attention is a scarce life resource. AIIMIN treats Focus and acute stress moments as **protected states**.

Rules:

- No modal coaching during Focus.
- Notifications must deserve attention (see Interaction Principles).
- Capture during urge moments must be shorter than the urge narrative, not longer.

## Learning & mastery

Power users get Command Palette, chords, and density. Beginners get defaults and one obvious path. Mastery is optional depth, not mandatory ceremony.

Empty states teach shortcuts. Onboarding teaches the loop, not the entire schema.

## Behavioral success metrics (philosophical)

| Signal | Interpretation |
|--------|----------------|
| Falling interaction count for same outcomes | Compression working |
| Rising journal funnel completion | Capture-first working |
| Stable Life Score usage without shame spikes | Honesty working |
| Export/delete used without fear campaigns | Trust working |

## Future impact

Wearables and passive sensing must obey the same ethics: opt-in, correctable, non-clinical, interruptible.

## Tradeoffs

Refusing shame and JITAI forgoes some short-term “engagement” tricks. Long-term trust is the bet.

## Known risks

- Gamification drift: XP theater swallowing honesty.
- Coach voice becoming therapist under “care” branding.
- Compression removing necessary high-stakes fields (meds) — safety fields are exempt from kill list.
