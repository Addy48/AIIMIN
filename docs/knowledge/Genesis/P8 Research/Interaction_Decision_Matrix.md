## Purpose

When two valid interaction principles conflict, taste cannot decide. This matrix is the **constitutional tie-breaker** for the Interaction Layer rewrite.

It is not a UI guide. It is not an implementation guide. It states **which principle wins by default**, **when exceptions apply**, and **why**.

If Language and Grammar disagree with this matrix on precedence, **this matrix governs conflicts among principles**; Language and Grammar govern vocabulary and composition. If any research artifact disagrees with P7 or frozen P8, **P7 and frozen P8 win**.

## Decision areas

### Capture vs Organization

**Competing principles**  
Immediate raw save (catch reflex) vs perfect categorization before persistence (organization-first).

**Default winner**  
**Capture.** Raw pulse MUST settle before organization is required. Organization arrives as Offer after Settle, never as gate before Settle.

**Exception(s)**  
None at ingress. Organization may block **Commit** of structured fields when stakes require accuracy (e.g., irreversible financial export) — but raw pulse remains caught and settled first. Review-time organization may be focal when human explicitly invoked Recall → Organize paragraph.

**Rationale**  
Under cognitive load, lost intent is worse than miscategorized intent. Correction is cheaper than prevention (Language correction model). Frozen capture law: ceremony-free save, universal convergence.

**References**  
GOV-066, GOV-126, GOV-028 · Language: Law of the Exhale, Law of the Offer · Grammar: minimum sentence `Catch → Settle`; invalid `Offer → Catch` · P8 Ch 06 Capture System

---

### Speed vs Certainty

**Competing principles**  
Minimize time-to-settle vs require explicit certainty before state change.

**Default winner**  
**Speed** for recoverable acts. Optimistic Settle with Hand-back.

**Exception(s)**  
**Certainty** wins when: (a) act is irreversible after Settle; (b) act affects privacy scope of another person; (c) act propagates beyond undo window; (d) governance mandates typed Veil. Certainty also wins **after** raw Settle when human is committing inferred structure they have seen (Adjust opportunity was present).

**Rationale**  
Speed serves relief after capture. Certainty serves trust on irreversible paths. The ladder climbs only when Hand-back cannot restore prior world (Language confirmation model).

**References**  
GOV-125, GOV-015, GOV-065, GOV-128 · Language: stakes ladder, Hand-back · Grammar: confirmation sentence template · P8 Ch 02 emotional contract

---

### AI Initiative vs User Sovereignty

**Competing principles**  
Proactive AI assistance vs human as final authority over life record.

**Default winner**  
**User sovereignty.** AI proposes; human dismisses, adjusts, acts, or ignores. No auto-commit of structured inference without visible Offer.

**Exception(s)**  
**AI initiative** as Parser and Linker **after Settle** — silent infrastructure that produces peripheral Offers, not focal speech. **Knock** permitted only when interruptibility window is open, provenance is present, and single action is offered. Clarifier may ask **one** question when inference is blocked and Kill List allows — never before first Settle on pulse.

**Rationale**  
Mixed-initiative AI is not authoritative AI. Coaching that removes dismiss path violates sovereignty invariant (Language AI model). Frozen AI architecture: roles bounded, interruptibility respected.

**References**  
GOV-033, GOV-051, GOV-137, GOV-139, GOV-141 · Language: sovereignty invariant, Knock eligibility · Grammar: AI sentences, Parser subordinate clause · P8 Ch 07 AI Architecture

---

### Automation vs Explainability

**Competing principles**  
Automatic structure application vs user-visible explanation of what happened and why.

**Default winner**  
**Explainability** for any automation that changes or proposes structured truth the user would notice. Automation without explainability is forbidden for user-visible mutations.

**Exception(s)**  
**Automation** without focal explanation for: (a) raw durability (catch/sync mechanics) when Hold is honest; (b) ranking and ordering in Recall results if provenance available on demand; (c) Kill List field omission — skipped fields are omitted, not silently filled. Parser automation MUST surface as Offer/chip or Hold — never invisible assign.

**Rationale**  
Silent wrongness destroys long-term trust (Pre-Design Study). Provenance is a trust pillar (Language trust model). High-confidence wrongness is worse than low-confidence honesty.

**References**  
GOV-051, GOV-132, GOV-137 · Language: Law of Provenance, uncertainty state · Grammar: uncertainty sentence · P8 Ch 07 confidence bands

---

### Recovery vs Prevention

**Competing principles**  
Prevent errors upfront (forms, confirms) vs allow errors and recover (undo, chips).

**Default winner**  
**Recovery** for all recoverable mistakes. Hand-back and chip Adjust over upfront interrogation.

**Exception(s)**  
**Prevention** via Veil (and Typed Veil at peak stakes) when Hand-back cannot restore prior world. Prevention also permitted as **Pause** on medium bulk recoverable scope — still with Hand-back after Pause.

**Rationale**  
Correction is always cheaper than prevention (Language correction model). Fear copy on recoverable acts is ungrammatical (Grammar mistakes catalog).

**References**  
GOV-128, GOV-126, GOV-015 · Language: Law of the Hand-back, Law of the Chip · Grammar: recovery paragraph · P8 Ch 02 friction philosophy

---

### Visual Beauty vs Truth

**Competing principles**  
Polish, aesthetics, expressive layout vs legible honest system state.

**Default winner**  
**Truth.** Settle, Hold, uncertainty, and failure states MUST remain legible regardless of visual treatment.

**Exception(s)**  
**Visual beauty** on Ritual tone surfaces (brand) when beauty does not obscure state on product paths. Beauty may enhance scan clarity (hierarchy) in Review — never substitute for status text or provenance.

**Rationale**  
Emotional contract requires honesty after capture. Fake settle destroys more trust than slow hold (Language trust model). Composition serves recognition, not decoration alone (GOV-106 spirit).

**References**  
GOV-108, GOV-132, GOV-087 · Language: Law of Settle, Law of Honest Hold · Grammar: Hold sentence · P8 Ch 02 never feel: confused by decoration

---

### Motion vs Latency

**Competing principles**  
Motion as feedback/orientation vs minimum time to durable Settle on capture path.

**Default winner**  
**Latency** on capture path. Motion MUST NOT delay successful capture Settle.

**Exception(s)**  
**Motion** after Settle on productivity paths (~150–250ms band) for feedback, continuity, hierarchy, state — never before raw Settle. Motion on Ritual surfaces may be longer if capture path unaffected. Reduced motion: instant state with alternate cue — motion yields entirely to accessibility.

**Rationale**  
Capture is sacred. Perform → Settle is grammar violation. Motion communicates; it does not gate ingress (Pre-Design Study, frozen motion governance).

**References**  
GOV-116, GOV-063, GOV-072, GOV-121 · Language: Catch → Settle spine · Grammar: rhythm exhale at Settle; invalid Perform → Settle · P8 Ch 06 cross-ref

---

### Density vs Readability

**Competing principles**  
Information per screen vs calm scannable comprehension.

**Default winner**  
**Readability** in Breath and Scan tones. Density is mode-bound, not global.

**Exception(s)**  
**Density** increases in Command tone (palette, search, triage) when human invoked recall. Density increases in Orient during Act on complex entities if anchor remains singular. Brand Ritual tone may be sparse-expressive, not dense.

**Rationale**  
Density matches cognitive mode (GOV-109). Command density on capture surface is a grammar mistake (Grammar mistakes: command tone on breath).

**References**  
GOV-109, GOV-032 · Language: tones Breath · Scan · Command · Ritual · Grammar: tension map · P8 Ch 08 surface jobs

### Keyboard vs Touch

**Competing principles**  
Keyboard-accelerated workflows vs touch-first/platform-native interaction.

**Default winner**  
**Neither globally.** **Grammar is canonical**; input modality adapts. Same sentences, same outcomes, same verbs.

**Exception(s)**  
**Touch** wins for platform chrome: system back, share sheet, biometrics. **Keyboard** wins as accelerator only — never the sole path to Catch. If workflow differs by modality, the workflow with **fewer steps to Settle** on breath surfaces is canonical; the other modality MUST converge to same Outcome.

**Rationale**  
Progressive enhancement (GOV-129). Platform body, AIIMIN soul (Language Law of Platform Body). Primitive contract across clients (frozen Ch 01).

**References**  
GOV-129, GOV-134, GOV-040, GOV-046 · Language: keyboard/touch philosophy · Grammar: domain-agnostic sentences · P8 Ch 01 Primitive

### Beginner vs Expert

**Competing principles**  
Defaults that teach and tolerate discovery vs defaults that maximize expert throughput.

**Default winner**  
**Beginner-safe defaults** with **expert acceleration available**. Same UI; experts gain chords and palette — not a separate product.

**Exception(s)**  
**Expert speed** in Command tone when human explicitly entered command paragraph (palette, logger). Expert shortcuts MUST NOT remove visible anchor on Breath surfaces. No mandatory onboarding call to unlock capture.

**Rationale**  
Progressive mastery (Language). Mastery is accelerative, not configurational. Superhuman-style expertise tax is rejected (Pre-Design Study).

**References**  
GOV-129, GOV-032 · Language: progressive mastery, discoverability · Grammar: command paragraph · P8 Ch 02

### Consistency vs Context

**Competing principles**  
Global interaction consistency vs surface-appropriate behavior.

**Default winner**  
**Consistency** at grammar layer: same sentences, verbs, chips, Settle/Hold honesty, sovereignty rules across all surfaces.

**Exception(s)**  
**Context** at tone and anchor layer: Breath on capture surfaces, Scan on review, Command when invoked, Ritual on brand. Surface job (frozen Ch 08) selects tone and anchor — not alternate grammar. Mobile web `/m` device ceiling is product lock — context mandated by platform spec, not taste.

**Rationale**  
Recognizability requires verb truth (Language Law of Verb Truth). Context changes cognitive mode, not compositional law.

**References**  
GOV-109, GOV-131, GOV-013, GOV-041 · Language: tones · Grammar: universal patterns table · P8 Ch 08, Ch 04

### Privacy vs Convenience

**Competing principles**  
Frictionless flow vs protection of personal and shared life data.

**Default winner**  
**Privacy** on any act that exposes, exports, deletes, or shares data across trust boundaries. Convenience wins on private recoverable capture and recall within user's own scope.

**Exception(s)**  
**Convenience** for: ceremony-free catch of user's own pulse; optimistic commit with Hand-back; single-field chip adjust without re-auth. **Privacy** always wins for: family shared entities, finance export, account deletion, credential surfaces — Typed Veil where mandated.

**Rationale**  
Life data is precious. Branded destructive confirm (GOV-065). Emotional contract includes protected feel. Convenience never trades away informed consent on irreversible or shared scope.

**References**  
GOV-015, GOV-065, GOV-033 · Language: Veil, Typed Veil · Grammar: irreversible sentence · P8 Ch 02, Ch 08 Family/Finance surfaces

### AI Confidence vs AI Helpfulness

**Competing principles**  
Ship helpful AI output vs withhold when confidence is low.

**Default winner**  
**Honest confidence over false helpfulness.** Uncertain inference MUST present as uncertain Offer — soft chip, provenance, dismiss path. Abstain from focal Knock when confidence cannot support provenance.

**Exception(s)**  
**Helpfulness** as raw catch always succeeds — Parser failure still yields Settled pulse without structure. Clarifier asks one question only when blocked entirely. Coach Knock requires confidence band sufficient for provenance per frozen AI law — otherwise latent.

**Rationale**  
High-confidence wrongness is worse than low-confidence honesty (Language uncertainty). Silent wrongness is forbidden. Helpfulness that lies is not help.

**References**  
GOV-137, GOV-051, GOV-053 · Language: uncertainty state, Clarifier rules · Grammar: uncertainty sentence · P8 Ch 07

---

### Interruption vs Flow

**Competing principles**  
Timely AI insight / system message vs uninterrupted human flow.

**Default winner**  
**Flow.** System remains silent at focal layer unless interruptibility window is open and Knock passes eligibility.

**Exception(s)**  
**Interruption** permitted as: (a) single Knock in open window with provenance; (b) Veil for stakes human initiated; (c) Hold surfaced honestly when durability pending on object human is viewing; (d) system-critical integrity failure — plain language, one focal message, recovery path. Never interrupt Breath Catch or Veil for coaching.

**Rationale**  
Interruptibility is respect (GOV-033). Interruption cost in attention seconds and trust units (Language). Knock stacking is ungrammatical.

**References**  
GOV-033, GOV-141, GOV-122 · Language: interruption model, Law of the Knock · Grammar: canonical interruption · P8 Ch 07 coaching window

---

### Completeness vs Cognitive Load

**Competing principles**  
Show all available information vs hide to preserve attention.

**Default winner**  
**Cognitive load** on Breath and Scan. Information defaults to latent or peripheral unless human invoked Recall or Act.

**Exception(s)**  
**Completeness** when: (a) human in Command paragraph (asked for density); (b) human in Veil (consequence must be complete); (c) human in Orient on entity they explicitly recalled; (d) provenance on active Offer or Knock — complete enough to correct, not exhaustive. Today surface shows one primary capture story — not widget completeness (GOV-106 spirit).

**Rationale**  
Latent discipline (Language Law of Latent Discipline). Attention layers: focal vs peripheral vs latent. Reduce decisions (GOV-123).

**References**  
GOV-123, GOV-032, GOV-106 · Language: attention model · Grammar: silence during breath · P8 Ch 08 Today surface

## Supplementary decisions

### Optimistic UI vs Sync Honesty

**Competing principles**  
Instant perceived completion vs accurate pending state.

**Default winner**  
**Sync honesty.** Hold when durability incomplete. Optimistic presentation permitted only when Hand-back remains valid and Hold is not mislabeled Settle.

**Exception(s)**  
Local Settle with visible Hold indicator for remote sync is honest — both may coexist if grammar distinguishes them.

**Rationale**  
GOV-132, GOV-108. Law of Honest Hold.

**References**  
GOV-132, GOV-089 · Language: Hold vs Settle · Grammar: waiting state

### One Primary Action vs Power User Density

**Competing principles**  
Single anchor vs many actions visible.

**Default winner**  
**One primary action** per moment.

**Exception(s)**  
Command tone after human invocation — secondary actions dense but **one anchor** remains (e.g., search field). Bulk Act uses Pause, not multiple anchors.

**Rationale**  
GOV-122. Law of the Anchor.

**References**  
GOV-122 · Language: Anchor noun · Grammar: invalid two anchors

### Chip vs Form

**Competing principles**  
Inline chip correction vs full form for field edit.

**Default winner**  
**Chip** for single-field inference correction.

**Exception(s)**  
**Form** (or multi-field veil) only when: adjusting one chip cannot express valid value space; stakes require Veil; accessibility alternative explicitly provided with same grammar outcome.

**Rationale**  
Law of the Chip. Adjust → form maze is invalid.

**References**  
GOV-126 · Language: correction model · Grammar: correction stack

### Engagement vs Dignity

**Competing principles**  
Streaks, nudges, rewards vs calm non-punitive presence.

**Default winner**  
**Dignity.** No shame empty states, no streak casino on calm surfaces, no nag loops.

**Exception(s)**  
**Proportional completion** acknowledgment on Act → Complete — brief, scan tone, no engagement extraction.

**Rationale**  
GOV-033, GOV-030. Law of Dignity. Anti-casino motion (GOV-055 spirit).

**References**  
GOV-030, GOV-033, GOV-055 · Language: completion canonical · Grammar: invalid casino completion

### Thread Visibility vs Capture Purity

**Competing principles**  
Show connections at ingress vs keep capture minimal.

**Default winner**  
**Capture purity.** Threads latent at ingress; visible in Offer as optional chips after Settle, focal in Reflect/Recall.

**Exception(s)**  
Linker may propose thread-chip in peripheral Offer — dismissible, never required for Commit.

**Rationale**  
Law of the Thread. Graph homework at ingress forbidden.

**References**  
Language: Thread noun, Law of the Thread · Grammar: organize pattern · P8 Ch 05 graph cross-ref

### Platform Convention vs AIIMIN Verb

**Competing principles**  
OS-native behavior vs cross-client verb consistency.

**Default winner**  
**Platform convention** for navigation chrome, back, share, biometrics.

**Exception(s)**  
**AIIMIN verb** for life acts: catch, adjust, settle, dismiss, hand-back — identical meaning on web, native, desktop.

**Rationale**  
Law of Platform Body + Law of Verb Truth.

## Precedence hierarchy

When multiple principles conflict across domains, resolve in this order — **higher wins**:

```text
Tier 0 — P7 Governance (GOV-*)
Tier 1 — Frozen P8 Chapters 01–08
Tier 2 — Human sovereignty (dismiss, adjust, hand-back, ignore)
Tier 3 — Truth (Settle, Hold, provenance, verb truth)
Tier 4 — Catch reflex (ceremony-free raw save)
Tier 5 — Emotional contract (relief · clarity · agency)
Tier 6 — Privacy / irreversible protection (Veil ladder)
Tier 7 — Interaction Decision Matrix (this document)
Tier 8 — Interaction Language (laws, vocabulary)
Tier 9 — Interaction Grammar (composition, rhythm)
Tier 10 — Interaction Layer Pre-Design Study (research, non-binding)
Tier 11 — Taste, trend, competitor mimicry
```

**Collisions within Tier 7–9:** Apply the specific decision entry in this matrix first. If no entry exists, apply Language hierarchy (sovereignty → truth → catch → emotional contract → anchor → platform → tone → acceleration). If still tied, **choose lower cognitive load on Breath/Scan** and **higher explicitness on Veil**.

## Non-negotiable principles

These outcomes are **never** inverted by exception in interaction design:

**Raw pulse MUST Settle (or honestly Hold) before organization is required.**

**User MUST always be able to dismiss any Offer or Knock without penalty.**

**Recoverable acts MUST NOT use fear copy or Typed Veil.**

**Irreversible acts MUST NOT Settle without Veil.**

**Hold MUST NOT present as Settle.**

**Knock MUST NOT occur during Breath Catch or Veil.**

**Motion MUST NOT delay capture Settle.**

**Auto-applied structured mutation without visible Offer is forbidden.**

**Chat thread MUST NOT be the system of record for life.**

**Two focal anchors in one moment are forbidden.**

**Chord-only or palette-only capture is forbidden.**

**Generic system confirm for product destructive paths is forbidden.**

**Shame-based empty or error states are forbidden.**

**Re-knock of dismissed suggestion in same session is forbidden.**

Any design that requires violating a non-negotiable is **unconstitutional** — escalate to Founder ADR, not design iteration.

## Conflict resolution algorithm

Use this sequence when principles conflict in design review:

**Step 1 — Classify stakes**  
Recoverable? Irreversible? Shared/privacy scope? If irreversible or shared → escalate to Veil rules immediately.

**Step 2 — Identify cognitive mode**  
Breath, Scan, Command, or Ritual? Apply density and interruption defaults for that mode.

**Step 3 — Write the grammar sentence**  
Can the flow be written as valid sentence + paragraph? If not, flow is rejected regardless of aesthetics.

**Step 4 — Lookup matrix**  
Find decision area. Apply default winner unless exception condition is met **explicitly**.

**Step 5 — Check non-negotiables**  
Any violation → reject. No exception.

**Step 6 — Apply precedence hierarchy**  
If matrix silent, walk Tiers 2–9 top-down until one principle clearly dominates.

**Step 7 — Document residual tension**  
If legitimate exception used, record: which principle lost, which exception clause, which surface/mode. Required input for Ch 09 authoring.

**Step 8 — Founder escalation**  
If Tiers 0–1 are silent and matrix + Language + Grammar do not resolve → Founder Decision Block. Do not ship on taste.

## Amendment policy

**Who may amend**  
Founder only. Agents and contributors may propose amendments; they do not take effect without explicit founder ratification.

**What may be amended**  
Default winners, exception clauses, and supplementary decisions in this matrix. Non-negotiables change only via P7 governance amendment or Founder ADR with P8 chapter impact assessment.

**What may not be amended here**  
P7 GOV decisions. Frozen P8 chapters. Vocabulary in Interaction Language (amend Language document separately, then reconcile matrix).

**Amendment process**  
Propose written delta: competing principles, old default, new default, rationale, governance impact. Run conflict resolution algorithm on three representative flows. Update Changelog below. If Ch 09–12 already drafted, flag rewrite required.

**Versioning**  
This document is research until Ch 09–12 rewrite cites it. After rewrite ships, matrix becomes **referenced authority** in P8 Architecture Ledger — still subordinate to P7 and frozen chapters.

**Sunset**  
When Ch 09–12 freeze, matrix content SHOULD be absorbed into Chapter 09 as precedence section — this research file remains historical record; Chapter 09 becomes operational cite.
