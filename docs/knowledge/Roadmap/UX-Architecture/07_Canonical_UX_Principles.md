## C-UX-01 — One Life OS, not glued apps

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Purpose** | Keep AIIMIN one connected life graph in UX structure — domains may exist; they must not behave as separate products. |
| **Genesis traceability** | P5 Exec Summary twenty-year #1 — Personal Life OS, not twelve apps; IA-1 Graph over folders (`Genesis/P5 Constitution/00_EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md`, `06_INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE_PRINCIPLES.md`) |
| **Supporting evidence** | Program 0: broad multi-domain surface strength + constitution↔UI gap (Readiness Report). Intelligence: many domain surfaces (Surface/Feature inventories) with IA orphans and dual hubs (D03, D04). |
| **Constraints** | Architecture may not invent new top-level product apps. MERGE/KEEP decisions must reduce fragmentation, not add parallel OSes. |
| **Validation** | Cross-links and shared primitives visible in architecture docs; no “app within app” ceilings without Genesis cite. |
| **Failure cases** | Finance/Family/Career feel like unrelated products; orphan Insights/Identity with no graph relation; duplicate settings hubs left as permanent dual OS. |
