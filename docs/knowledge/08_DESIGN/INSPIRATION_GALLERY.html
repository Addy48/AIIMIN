1. UX Diagnosis: Why Current UI Feels "Too AI"

The Drafting Table palette has great conceptual rigor, but user testing and visual audits reveal critical psychological friction points.

The 4 Core Root Causes of the "AI Tool" Perception

  • Steel Blue (#749DC4) Accent Overuse: Steel blue is the signature tint of ChatGPT and Claude web interfaces. Because it dominates AIIMIN's headers, icons, borders, and radar charts, users immediately categorize AIIMIN as an AI chatbot wrapper rather than an autonomous Life Operating System.
  • Flat Elevation Plane (#141414): Currently, cards, modals, sheets, and the main background sit on the exact same flat charcoal plane with 1px border outlines (#333333). This lacks optical depth and creates visual fatigue during extended daily use.
  • Monospace Typographic Overload: Monospaced fonts are essential for numbers, financial sums, and timestamps. However, when used across body copy, labels, and titles, the interface feels like an unstyled Linux terminal debug console.
  • Clinical Feedback vs Emotional Stakes: Forms and surveys (1-5 mood numbers, static checkboxes) feel like medical surveys. Leading apps (Duolingo, Habitica, Daylio) use visceral feedback (animated flames, HP damage, expressive glyphs) to make habit formation feel consequential.
