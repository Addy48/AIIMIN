## Product Overview

**One-liner:** AIIMIN is the personal Life OS that turns daily Capture into linked truth — habits, money, focus, mood, family — with AI that Offers structure and never silently rewrites your life.

**What it does:** One account across a desktop web Life OS (command, reports, vault) and a native Android companion (capture, health, money review, day loop). Server-computed Life Score across BODY · MIND · DISCIPLINE · MONEY · MOOD. Waitlist + founding economics now; go-live targeting end of October 2026.

**Product category:** Personal Life OS / behavioural operating system (not habit RPG, not wellness pet, not generic AI dashboard).

**Product type:** B2C SaaS (freemium Explore + paid Core/Pro/Elite) · India-first · Americas USD catalog proposed.

**Business model:** Explore ₹0 forever · Core ₹29/mo · Pro ₹59 list / ₹49 founding · Elite ₹99 list / ₹79 founding (waitlist). USD proposed $0 / $3 / $7 / $16 with founding discounts — not live Stripe geo yet.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Life tools are siloed; “AI wellness” apps either shame, cosplay RPG, or silently rewrite your data.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Notion = docs, not daily Capture grammar
- Habitica / Finch = progression costume ≠ life graph
- Pure finance apps ignore mood/discipline; pure habit apps ignore UPI reality

**What it costs them:** Context-switching time, lost streaks of truth, money without person context.

**Emotional tension:** Fear of being judged by the app; fear of losing private journal to “AI analysis.”

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** Habitica, Finch, generic habit trackers — fall short on money+family+server Life Score craft.  
**Secondary:** Notion + Todoist + Headspace stack — expensive, unlinked.  
**Indirect:** Spreadsheets / WhatsApp money notes — no OS, no compounding graph.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**
- Capture → Settle → typed links (life graph), not dashboard widgets
- AI Offer / Adjust / Commit — never silent write
- India-native money path (UPI review) without being India-only forever
- Server Life Score five dimensions — clients never invent XP
- Web deep OS + native companion (not phone-web fake analytics)

**How we do it differently:** Linking + provenance over feature sprawl.  
**Why that's better:** Patterns compound; trust rises.  
**Why customers choose us:** One system; founding rates; honest about Android closed testing.
